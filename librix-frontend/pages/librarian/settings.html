About Multi-Tenancy (RBAC)

LibriX isolates book collections, checkouts, and patron records per Organization ID. When you add a new title, it will automatically link to your selected Organization ID.

Current Role Privileges:
  • Add books with automatic Org ID assignment
  • Delete & update books belonging to active Org ID
  • Circulate books to enrolled organization patrons
  • View real-time organization metrics & capacity
