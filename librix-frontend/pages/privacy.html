Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and optional profile details such as phone number, address, and bio. When you borrow books, we record circulation data including issue dates, due dates, returns, renewals, and fines.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to operate the library: managing your borrowing history, sending due-date reminders, processing reservations, calculating fines, and showing personalized book recommendations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Account credentials are stored using industry-standard hashing. Session tokens are issued as signed JWTs. Uploaded files such as profile pictures are stored on the server and served only through the application.

4. Your Rights

You may update your profile information at any time from your dashboard. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact the library administration through the Contact page.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
