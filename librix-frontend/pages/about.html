OUR VISION

Transforming How Communities Read

LibriX was created to modernize fragmented library operations and empower readers with transparent reading analytics.

THE PROBLEM

The Challenge of Traditional Libraries

Manual record keeping, scattered catalogs, and lack of real-time inventory make it exhausting for readers to locate books and difficult for administrators to track due dates, handle fines, and prevent stock loss.

Furthermore, readers often pick books that are either too elementary or overly dense because standard catalog cards provide no objective measure of reading difficulty or expected time commitment.

THE LIBRIX SOLUTION

Smart Intelligence Meets Circulation

LibriX unifies all core library operations—catalogue search, single-click borrowings, digital reservations, automated renewals, fine ledgers, and patron reviews—into one clean web application.

ALGORITHMIC INSIGHT

How the Readability Engine Works

LibriX analyzes text patterns using proven linguistic formulas to give you objective reading metrics.

📐

Flesch Reading Ease

Calculates a score between 0 and 100 based on average sentence length and syllables per word. 90-100 is comic-strip simple; 30-50 is academic and collegiate.

🎓

Flesch-Kincaid Grade

Translates syllable density and sentence complexity into a US grade reading level (e.g., Grade 8.2), making it simple for educators and students to calibrate.

⏱️

Reading Pace Projection

Computes precise reading completion time based on 200 words-per-minute average adult silent comprehension rates, allowing readers to plan their schedule.
