Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I borrow a book? Standard loan period is 14 days with up to 2 renewals if no other patron has reserved the title.
How are overdue fines handled? Overdue fines accumulate at $0.50/day after the grace period and can be paid or waived via the admin desk.
How does Readability scoring work? Our background analyzer scans word and sentence syllables using the Flesch-Kincaid formula to estimate reading ease.
